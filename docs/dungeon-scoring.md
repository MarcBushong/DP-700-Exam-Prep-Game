# Honest scores under the dungeon theme

An encounter awards **one point** for the complete correct answer and **zero
points** for an incorrect or unanswered answer. Multi-select requires the exact
set, with no duplicate choices. Partial-credit jokes or animations never award
partial credit. Response time is reported, not used to change the technical score.

The percentage is `100 * correct / questions in the run`, rounded for display.
It is an in-game practice result, not an official exam scaled score, pass
prediction, or certification eligibility decision.

## Floors, chambers, and dungeons

Floors are the published objective domains; chambers are the published skills
and subskills. Fantasy labels supplement the actual titles rather than replacing
the factual mapping. Results retain the credential and objective version used
for the run, so new content cannot silently redefine an old result.

A raid identifies each encounter's source dungeon. Its per-dungeon scores use
only that dungeon's encounters and taxonomy. Similar-looking skill names in
different credentials must not merge practice evidence. A combined raid score
does not imply that the hero is ready for every credential in the raid.

Small samples must remain visible. Strong results on one or two encounters do
not establish mastery of a floor, class, product area, or credential. Weak-area
recommendations must state the actual missed skills and sample sizes that
motivated them. No observed mistakes means no invented weak area.

## Readiness is a content gate

The default Study Run threshold is **25 verified encounters**, coverage of all
major floors, and no blocking content failure. The default Boss Gauntlet
threshold is **75 verified encounters**, sufficient objective breadth for varied
runs, hard encounters, and no major coverage gaps. These are configurable
product thresholds, not official vendor exam rules.

An active credential can still have an unavailable or limited content package.
A sealed credential cannot be opened merely because a question file exists.
Readiness must be calculated from eligible reviewed content, not a catalog
count copied by hand. Stale, rejected, manual-review, rubric-failing, or
otherwise blocked encounters do not contribute to playable readiness.

Published objective weights govern weighted selection where available and
feasible. If weights are unavailable, disclose the allocation strategy rather
than inventing percentages. A short or heavily filtered run may not realize
the published ranges; disclose that limitation instead of calling it balanced.

## Cosmetic progress

HP, XP, levels, loot, streak effects, and boss framing are presentation over
the same transparent technical metrics. Losing HP must not alter a correct
answer, secretly change a score, delete history, or end a run before the chosen
quiz rules require it.

The cosmetic calculation awards 10 XP and 10 damage per correct response.
Level is `floor(XP / 100) + 1`; loot is `floor(correct / 5)`. HP starts at 100
and loses 10 for each incorrect or unanswered submitted response, bounded at
zero. Duplicate response IDs are counted once. These numbers are game rules,
not claims about certification progress.

DM chattiness and reduced motion control presentation only. Boss Gauntlet runs
withhold correctness, explanations, and answer-revealing narration until
completion. Any configurable timer not grounded in official duration data must
be labelled a generic practice timer.

## Persistence and lifecycle

Progress stays in the current browser. Existing DP-700 result snapshots retain
their original answers and scores during migration. Retrying uses currently
eligible reviewed encounters, while reviewing an old run uses the saved facts
and objective version.

Unknown historical metadata must be labelled as legacy or unavailable; never
invent a past objective version or retroactively mark an old snapshot verified.
Retiring a dungeon or refreshing objectives must not delete the hero's history.
